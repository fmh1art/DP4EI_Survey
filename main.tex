\begin{abstract}
Data preparation, which aims to transform heterogeneous and noisy raw tables into analysis-ready data, remains a major bottleneck in data science.
Recent approaches leverage large language models (LLMs) to automate data preparation from natural language specifications. However, existing LLM-powered methods either make decisions without grounding in intermediate execution results, or rely on linear interaction processes that offer limited support for revising earlier decisions.
To address these limitations, we propose \sys, an LLM-powered agentic system for autonomous data preparation. \sys constructs data preparation pipelines through iterative, execution-grounded interaction with an environment that materializes intermediate table states and returns runtime feedback. To overcome the limitations of linear interaction, \sys organizes pipeline construction with tree-based agentic reasoning, enabling structured exploration and non-local revision based on execution feedback.
To enable effective learning of such behaviors, we propose a progressive agentic training framework, together with data synthesis that supplies diverse and complex ADP tasks.
Extensive experiments show that \sys achieves data preparation accuracy comparable to strong closed-source models (\eg GPT-5) while incurring 15$\times$ lower inference cost, while establishing state-of-the-art performance among open-source baselines and generalizing effectively across diverse datasets.


%Autonomous data preparation (ADP) is critical for reducing the time-consuming burden of transforming heterogeneous and noisy raw tables into analysis-ready data. While recent Large Language Model (LLM) based agents show promise, they typically rely on linear, append-only reasoning trajectories that lack sufficient grounding in intermediate execution results, making it difficult to recover from early errors in complex pipelines. To address these limitations, we propose \sys, an LLM-powered agentic framework that constructs data preparation pipelines through iterative planning, orchestration, and execution. Unlike existing ReAct-style methods, \sys employs a tree-based agentic reasoning mechanism, allowing the agent to systematically explore the solution space, backtrack upon execution failures, and revise decisions based on runtime feedback. To effectively optimize the agent, we introduce a progressive agentic training strategy that stages learning from simple operator usage to full tree-based reasoning, augmented by synthesized tasks derived from NL2SQL benchmarks to overcome data scarcity. Extensive experiments demonstrate that \sys achieves data preparation accuracy comparable to closed-source frontier models (e.g., GPT-5) at 57× lower inference cost, while establishing state-of-the-art performance among open-source baselines with strong cross-domain generalization.
