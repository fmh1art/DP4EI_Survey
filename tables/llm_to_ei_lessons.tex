Fixed-pool data competition ~\cite{li2024datacomp_2406_11794,gadre2023datacomp_2304_14108}
& Freeze the raw pool, learner, compute budgets, and evaluation. Vary only the data pipeline.
& A controlled robot-data track submits episode or segment membership, weights, roles, lineage, and preparation cost to fixed lightweight and foundation VLA learners.
& Robot utility needs simulation and blinded real tiers. Robot hours, reset labor, and safety prevent a fully cheap, deterministic leaderboard. \\
\midrule
Auditable filtering and deduplication ~\cite{penedo2023refinedweb_2306_01116,penedo2024fineweb_2406_17557}
& Version every rule and model gate, record rejection rates, deduplicate before splitting, and validate each pipeline change at fixed compute.
& Cascade technical checks, cross-modal alignment, joint state--action deduplication, and lineage-based leakage tests.
& Text documents are nearly exchangeable units. Adjacent transitions and segments have causal order, and visually similar episodes may contain distinct contact dynamics. \\
\midrule
Domain reweighting and resampling ~\cite{xie2023doremi_2305_10429,xie2023data_2302_03169}
& Learn mixture weights from a proxy learner or target distribution instead of setting them entirely by intuition.
& Hierarchical source, task, skill, and embodiment weights, with minimum sampling shares for each stage and transfer to a larger VLA.
& Domains evolve with the deployed policy. Weights learned by a small proxy model or in simulation may not transfer to the target model and robot, while small safety groups must not receive zero mass. \\
\midrule
Targeted instruction selection ~\cite{xia2024less_2402_04333,liu2023what_2312_15685}
& Estimate target influence and jointly consider quality, complexity, and diversity. A small targeted subset can outperform indiscriminate use in the reported language settings.
& Select complementary trajectories or segments for a task, learner, and budget. Retain difficulty and recovery coverage instead of ranking by one quality score.
& Scores computed independently for each example ignore delayed outcomes and complementarity among groups. As deployment changes which states are observed, a once-influential episode can become stale. \\
\midrule
Joint example and batch selection ~\cite{evans2024data_2406_17711}
& Value depends on interactions within a candidate batch, not only independent example scores.
& Select coherent approach--grasp--place groups or success--failure--recovery sets while controlling task and embodiment coverage.
& Arbitrary transition shuffling destroys executable sequences. Selection must respect hierarchical groups and episode-level split boundaries. \\
\midrule
Self-judged iterative alignment ~\cite{yuan2024self_2401_10020,shumailov2024collapse}
& A model can propose, judge, and retrain on new supervision, but recursive replacement of natural data can erase distribution tails.
& Generate counterfactual failures or preferences, calibrate a robot reward model, and iterate while retaining audited real demonstrations and deployment evidence.
& A shared generator and judge can ratify their own physical errors. The loop needs retained audited real data, independent verifiers, minimum sampling shares, small controlled training checks, and rollback. \\
