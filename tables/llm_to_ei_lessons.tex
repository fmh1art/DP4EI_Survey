Fixed-pool data competition
~\cite{li2024datacomp_2406_11794,gadre2023datacomp_2304_14108}
& Freeze the raw pool, learner, compute budgets, and evaluation; vary only the
data pipeline.
& A DataComp-Robot track submits episode/segment membership, weights, roles,
lineage, and preparation cost to fixed lightweight and foundation VLA learners.
& Robot utility needs simulation and blinded real tiers; robot hours, reset
labor, and safety prevent a fully cheap, deterministic leaderboard. \\
\midrule
Auditable filtering/deduplication
~\cite{penedo2023refinedweb_2306_01116,penedo2024fineweb_2406_17557}
& Version every rule/model gate, record rejection rates, deduplicate before
splitting, and validate each pipeline change at fixed compute.
& Cascade technical checks, cross-modal alignment, joint state--action
deduplication, and lineage-based leakage tests.
& Text documents are nearly exchangeable units; adjacent transitions and
segments have causal order, and visually similar episodes may contain distinct
contact dynamics. \\
\midrule
Domain reweighting/resampling
~\cite{xie2023doremi_2305_10429,xie2023data_2302_03169}
& Learn mixture weights from a proxy learner or target distribution instead of
setting them entirely by intuition.
& Hierarchical source/task/skill/embodiment weights, with stage-specific floors
and transfer to a larger VLA.
& Domains evolve with the deployed policy; proxy-to-target and sim-to-real
weight transfer can fail, while tiny safety groups must not receive zero mass. \\
\midrule
Targeted instruction selection
~\cite{xia2024less_2402_04333,liu2023what_2312_15685}
& Estimate target influence and jointly consider quality, complexity, and
diversity; a small targeted subset can outperform indiscriminate use in the
reported language settings.
& Select complementary trajectories/segments for a task, learner, and budget;
retain difficulty and recovery coverage instead of ranking by one quality
score.
& Pointwise gradients ignore temporal credit and group complementarity;
policy-induced state shift makes a once-influential episode stale. \\
\midrule
Joint example/batch selection
~\cite{evans2024data_2406_17711}
& Value depends on interactions within a candidate batch, not only independent
example scores.
& Select coherent approach--grasp--place groups or success--failure--recovery
sets while controlling task and embodiment coverage.
& Arbitrary transition shuffling destroys executable sequences; selection must
respect hierarchical groups and causal split boundaries. \\
\midrule
Self-judged iterative alignment
~\cite{yuan2024self_2401_10020,shumailov2024collapse}
& A model can propose, judge, and retrain on new supervision, but recursive
replacement of natural data can erase distribution tails.
& Generate counterfactual failures or preferences, calibrate a robot reward
model, and iterate with immutable real demonstrations and deployment audits.
& A shared generator/judge can ratify its own physical errors; real anchors,
independent verifiers, mixture floors, canaries, and rollback are mandatory. \\
