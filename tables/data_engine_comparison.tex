System & Gap and feedback signal & Data action and model update & Control mechanism and remaining limit \\
\midrule
RoboCat / AutoRT ~\cite{bousmalis2023robocat_2306_11706,ahn2024autort_2401_12963}
& New-task practice outcomes, foundation-model task proposals, physical feasibility, and safety checks.
& Generate practice or fleet experience, add it to a generalist mixture, and fine-tune. AutoRT separates semantic task proposal from executable robot collection.
& Safety rules and human or environment constraints gate collection. The loop does not autonomously attribute gains among proposer, data, objective, and model changes. \\
\midrule
$\pi^{*}_{0.6}$/RECAP ~\cite{intelligence20250_2511_14759}
& Terminal success and failure labels, time-to-outcome values, autonomous trials, and optional human interventions.
& Train a language-conditioned value function on accumulated experience and train the VLA to favor actions with higher predicted value. Collection and offline updates then repeat.
& Policy and value models are restarted from the audited pretrained checkpoint at each iteration in the reported implementation to limit drift. Rewards and interventions still require task-specific human infrastructure. \\
\midrule
DexFlyWheel ~\cite{zhu2025dexflywheel_2509_23829}
& Simulator reward and rollout success under new objects and configurations.
& One human seed bootstraps imitation learning. Reinforcement learning then adjusts the imitation policy through exploration, and successful rollouts and geometric augmentation form the next iteration's dataset.
& Closed-loop simulation makes validity testable, but manual reward design and the simulation model define the reachable distribution. It does not discover unanticipated failures in the real world. \\
\midrule
SOP ~\cite{pan2026sop_2601_03044}
& Fresh failures and interventions produced by the current policy, together with recent online and offline training losses.
& A distributed actor fleet streams complete episodes to one learner. A task-balanced sampler mixes static and online buffers, and human-intervention or RECAP updates are broadcast asynchronously.
& Uniform task weights and a clipped 0.2--0.8 online ratio preserve coverage. Checkpoints change only between episodes. It still depends on human interventions or task rewards and is tested over hours, not lifelong operation. \\
\midrule
LWD ~\cite{wang2026learning_2605_00416}
& Sparse terminal rewards over successes, failures, retries, partial progress, and intervention segments from a 16-robot fleet.
& A value model predicts a distribution of possible returns from both offline and online experience. Its gradients update the action-generation module, and the shared policy is repeatedly redeployed.
& A fixed offline reference, two value estimates combined conservatively, mixed replay, and freezing the pretrained vision--language part of the policy during online updates reduce instability. Four-hour studies do not yet establish long-term verifier stability or stability of the data distribution. \\
\midrule
Robot-Powered Data Flywheels ~\cite{grannen2025robot_2511_19647}
& Deployment novelty and errors, with environmental or catalog signals that can provide labels during use.
& A two-week library deployment collects task-relevant robot observations and uses them to adapt an upstream visual foundation model, feeding improved perception back to the robot system.
& Demonstrates that a flywheel may update perception rather than only the action policy. Site-specific labels and limited duration leave transfer and long-run bias unresolved. \\
\midrule
DEED (Data-Efficient Post-Training and Experience-Driven Learning) ~\cite{sis2026closing_2607_20345}
& Deployment experience and failures reveal gaps in an offline demonstration set.
& Experience is converted into new or revised demonstrations, then used to update the policy and the next data-collection decision.
& Makes demonstration evolution explicit, but controlled comparisons before model replacement, independent verifiers, and rollback remain necessary when several loop components change together. \\
