$\pi_{0.5}\!\rightarrow\!\pi_{0.7}\!\rightarrow\!\pi^{*}_{0.6}$
~\cite{intelligence20250_2504_16054,intelligence20260_2604_15483,intelligence20250_2511_14759}
& $\pi_{0.5}$ separates mobile, multi-environment, cross-embodiment,

NVIDIA GR00T N1 and DreamGen/GR00T-Dreams
~\cite{nvidia2025gr00t_2503_14734,jang2025dreamgen_2505_12705,nvidia2026gr00t17}
& A pyramid places web/human video at the broad base, neural and simulated
trajectories in the middle, and embodiment-specific real demonstrations at the
top.  The later 1.7 documentation reports roughly 32k hours of real
demonstration/egocentric data and 8k hours of simulation.
& Latent-action or inverse-dynamics targets convert actionless/neural video;
prompt generation, re-captioning, and judging filter neural trajectories;
DexMimicGen supplies successful simulated trajectories.  N1 uses a 1:1
real:neural ratio in one neural post-training setting.  DreamGen turns a small
real seed into task-conditioned neural trajectories before robot post-training.
& N1 contains source and low-data experiments and reports a useful negative
case: narrow right-hand post-training erased a left-to-right handover skill.
Version-level scale from product documentation is engineering evidence, not a
controlled comparison with N1. \\

Gemini Robotics and Gemini Robotics 1.5
~\cite{team2025gemini_2503_20020,team2025gemini_2510_03342}
& The first report combines thousands of hours of ALOHA~2 demonstrations with
web, code, image/audio/video, embodied-reasoning, and VQA data; specialization
uses 2k--5k high-quality episodes per task.  Version 1.5 adds ALOHA, bi-arm
Franka, and Apollo humanoid data across thousands of tasks.
& Robot actions ground a pretrained multimodal model; relabeling connects
embodied reasoning to action prediction; narrow high-quality sets specialize
hard tasks.  Motion Transfer (MT) aligns heterogeneous robot motion, and the
same 1.5 checkpoint controls three embodiments.  Interleaved A/B/$n$ trials
reduce work-cell variance.
& The 1.5 report isolates single- versus multi-embodiment data and MT, a
stronger recipe ablation than most industrial reports.  It also states that
over 90\% of development evaluations were simulated.  Data quantities by
source, filtering thresholds, label lineage, and preparation cost are largely
closed. \\

Qwen-RobotManip
~\cite{yuan2026qwen_2606_17846}
& About 38.1k hours: more than 11k hours of robot demonstrations, 1,933 hours
of egocentric human video, and 24,808 hours synthesized across 15 robot
morphologies, plus a 28M-example vision--language mixture.
& A human-to-robot chain performs retargeting/smoothing, hand segmentation and
inpainting, base-pose/IK search, and depth compositing.  Five state--action
gates and three video--language--state checks precede an 80-D masked canonical
representation, camera-frame action alignment/CaPE, and embodiment/control
prompts.
& The report ablates action alignment, data scale, and synthetic sources and
evaluates both in- and out-of-distribution transfer.  It exposes source-level
defects, but the independent marginal value and physical error rate of the
24.8k synthetic hours are not fully identified. \\

Qwen-RobotWorld
~\cite{zhang2026qwen_2606_17030}
& The Embodied World Knowledge corpus contains 8.6M video--text clips and more
than 200M frames: 70\% embodied and 30\% general data, spanning over 20
embodiments and 500 action categories.
& Language is used as a shared action interface.  Domain-adaptive video
preprocessing, five-layer action annotation, and an LLM-judge/human-review loop
precede a General$+$Expert curriculum with four embodied phases.  The world
model can generate candidates for training, planning, and virtual evaluation.
& World-model and downstream demonstrations support generation and prediction
claims in the report.  They do not establish that every generated clip has
valid robot actions or positive policy-training utility; using one model as
both generator and uncalibrated judge would be circular. \\
