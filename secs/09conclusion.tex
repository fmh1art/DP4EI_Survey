\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Embodied intelligence does not learn from raw episode counts.  It learns from the
distribution produced after a sequence of often hidden decisions.  By organizing
those decisions along operation, signal, utility evidence, and lifecycle axes,
this survey connects acquisition, alignment, semantic enrichment, curation,
mixture design, synthesis, validation, and deployment feedback within one
problem.  The synthesis rejects two convenient simplifications.  Data quality is
not a policy-independent scalar, and generated plausibility is not physical or
training validity.

Industrial recipes sharpen this conclusion.  Qwen-RobotManip shows how
representation, motion, and behavior alignment enable scale.  The $\pi$ family
shows how training role and metadata can make mixed-quality experience usable.
GR00T assigns real anchors, simulation, and generated trajectories to different
tiers, while Gemini Robotics isolates cross-embodiment data from the mechanism
that aligns its motion.  None provides a universal recipe,
and their end-to-end scores are not a causal ranking of data operations.  Their
shared lesson is that a source becomes valuable through alignment, routing,
verification, and stage-specific use.

The most consequential shift is from one-shot cleaning to target-conditional
feedback control.  Failures and interventions make stronger evidence possible
because the deployed policy reveals its gaps, but they also introduce
feedback-dependent bias, error amplification, forgetting, verifier drift, and
unsafe
exploration.  LLM data engineering contributes evaluation on fixed candidate
pools, mixture learning, learner-aware selection, and warnings about recursive
generation.  Embodied learning adds action causality, physical validity, and
costly real feedback.  Progress requires calibrated links between proxies and
policy utility, explicit failure routing, retained real anchors, independent
verification, matched promotion comparisons, and joint rollback of data and
model versions.
Only after these controls are demonstrated should a predesigned data engine gain
the authority of an autonomous data-preparation agent.  Under that discipline,
data preparation becomes an independent, reproducible research layer for
embodied foundation models rather than an unreported collection of engineering
choices.
