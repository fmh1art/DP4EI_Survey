\section{Reproducible Review Protocol}
\label{app:protocol}

\subsection{Database Queries}

The archived DBLP discovery strings are the conjunction of a robotics context with each of the following phrases:
\begin{quote}\small
\texttt{robot data curation}, \texttt{robot demonstration selection}, \texttt{robot data quality}, \texttt{robot data filtering}, \texttt{robot data mixing}, \texttt{robot trajectory annotation segmentation}, \texttt{robot instruction relabeling}, \texttt{robot demonstration augmentation}, \texttt{robot synthetic demonstration generation}, \texttt{robot world model data generation}, \texttt{robot self-improvement data}, \texttt{robot online post-training}, \texttt{robot autonomous data collection}, \texttt{cross-embodiment action alignment}, \texttt{vision-language-action data engine}, and \texttt{embodied data preparation}.
\end{quote}

The arXiv queries restrict to \texttt{cat:cs.RO} and combine it with:
\begin{quote}\small
\texttt{"data curation"}, \texttt{"demonstration selection"}, \texttt{"data quality"}, \texttt{"data filtering"}, \texttt{"data mixing"}, \texttt{"automatic annotation" OR relabeling OR segmentation}, \texttt{"data augmentation" OR "synthetic demonstrations"}, \texttt{"world model" AND "data generation"}, \texttt{"self-improvement" OR "online post-training"}, \texttt{"autonomous data collection"}, \texttt{"cross-embodiment" OR "action alignment"}, and \texttt{"data engine"}.
\end{quote}

The artifact contains \texttt{scripts/search\_literature.py}, dated raw database responses, and query summaries that retain transient endpoint errors.
It also contains targeted retry files and both initial and resolved deduplicated discovery files in comma-separated-value format.
The resolved run contains 1,160 title-deduplicated candidates.
All eight initial query failures were recovered without overwriting the failed first-pass log.
Search-engine output is a discovery aid, not an automatic inclusion decision.
It is supplemented with backward and forward citations and checks of major labs, datasets, benchmarks, model reports, and official data documentation.

\subsection{Inclusion, Exclusion, and Version Rules}

A primary work is included if it changes the content, representation, membership, weight, role, or acquisition of embodied training data.
A work is also eligible if it proposes or evaluates a signal for those decisions, or reports enough of a system's data recipe to recover a preparation mechanism.
Human video, simulation, and generated data are included only when connected to embodied policy training.

Works are excluded if they only introduce a policy architecture, use an existing dataset without analyzing its preparation, present a generic simulator without a data operation, or concern an out-of-scope domain without a transferable mechanism.
Secondary summaries are not used to support technical claims.

ArXiv and subsequently published versions are one record.
The latest full text within the cutoff is read.
Verified published metadata is preferred for citation.
Technical-report versions are date-stamped because their scale and recipes can change.
Abstract-index records are not used for technical claims until promoted through primary-text reading.

\subsection{Coding Form}

Each core record is coded for its problem, original terminology, source, modality, preparation operation, transformed object, granularity, decision signal, and optimization objective.
Additional fields record human involvement, data and compute scale, intrinsic and proxy metrics, downstream simulation and real-robot evidence, and generalization across tasks, environments, learners, and embodiments.
The form also records assumptions, limitations, negative results, artifact availability, publication and evidence type, provenance, and verification scope.

The companion Chinese notes retain the paper-level synthesis used to produce these codes.
Quantitative results remain attributed to each paper's own setting.
No cross-paper ranking is computed when protocols differ.
